# Award Recognition: PeopleSoft 8 Hero Award

## 🌟 Overview & Significance

The **PeopleSoft 8 Hero Award** was presented to **Jim Pham** in recognition of exceptional personal commitment and technical contributions toward the successful, on-time release of **PeopleSoft 8**—the industry's first pure internet software architecture. 

![PeopleSoft 8 Hero Award](https://github.com/devservice-ora/projects/blob/main/Award-PSFT-PeopleSoft8HeroAward-2000.jpg)

---

## 🏆 Award Details

* **Award Title:** PeopleSoft 8 Hero
* **Organization:** PeopleSoft, Inc.
* **Recipient:** Jim Pham
* **Date:** October 11, 2000 (Grant Date: September 19, 2000)
* **Award Type:** Executive Recognition Letter & Equity Grant
* **Signatory:** Peter Gassner (GM, PeopleTools Technology)

---

### Key Highlights
* **Executive Commendation:** Signed by Peter Gassner, General Manager of PeopleTools Technology.
