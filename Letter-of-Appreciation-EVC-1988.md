# Recognition Letter: Evergreen Valley College Letter of Appreciation

## 🌟 Overview & Significance

This letter commends **Jim Pham** for his service as the **Media Officer of the Vietnamese Students Association (VSA)** at Evergreen Valley College over the 1987–1988 academic year. The letter highlights his transformative leadership and dedication, which revitalized student engagement and organization within the association.
