# Award Recognition: Ohlone College "Shining Star in Success" Award

## 🌟 Overview & Significance

The **Ohlone College "Shining Star in Success" Award** recognizes teaching excellence and outstanding contributions to student accomplishment. Presented to **Jim Pham** in 2016, this honor highlights dedication to high-quality instruction, innovative computer science and software engineering pedagogy, and sustained commitment to guiding students toward academic and career achievement.
