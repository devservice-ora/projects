# Award Recognition: Ohlone College Adjunct Service Award (15 Years)

## 🌟 Overview & Significance

The **Adjunct Service Award Recognition** honors **Jim Pham** for reaching a 15-year milestone of dedicated teaching and academic service as an Adjunct Professor of Computer Science at Ohlone College. Commencing his tenure in January 1998, this award reflects continuous leadership, excellence in computer science education, and longstanding commitment to student success.
